Lab 3 · the room

What the camera is for

Presage SmartSpectra reads pulse and breathing from the light reflected off your face. Frames are processed on this laptop by the SDK's native engine and are never written to disk.

  • Never recorded. No video, no frames, no face landmarks are stored.
  • Never uploaded by this app. There is no account and no cloud sync.
  • Stored locally: per-question summaries only — think time, peak pulse vs your baseline, whether you settled — in this app's data folder.
  • One network call: the SDK contacts Presage to validate the API key and compute metrics.

Your choices

The camera turns on only after you press the button. You can stop at any time and delete everything from Settings.
